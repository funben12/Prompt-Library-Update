Installed

Prompt Library Pro is now in your Applications folder.

The first time you open it, macOS may say it cannot check the app for malware, because it is not notarised by Apple. This is expected:

  1. Open Applications, right-click (or Control-click) Prompt Library Pro, and choose Open.
  2. Click Open again in the warning. You only need to do this once.
  3. If macOS asks to allow access to your Documents folder, click Allow. That is where your library lives.

Still blocked? Open Terminal and run:
xattr -cr "/Applications/Prompt Library Pro.app"

Want it on your phone or tablet? Open the app, click App tour at the bottom of the sidebar, and follow the phone and Tailscale steps.
