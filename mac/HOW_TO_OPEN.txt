Prompt Library Pro is not notarised with Apple, so macOS will refuse to
open it the normal way (double-click) the first time. This is expected
for an app distributed outside the App Store without a paid Apple
Developer certificate, not a broken download.

To open it:

1. Copy "Prompt Library Pro.app" out of this disk image and into
   Applications (or anywhere on your Mac).
2. Right-click (or Control-click) the app icon and choose "Open".
3. A dialog will warn the app is from an unidentified developer.
   Click "Open" again to confirm. You only need to do this once --
   after that it opens normally, including via double-click.

If step 2 still shows "can't be opened" or the app looks empty, macOS
has quarantined the download. Open Terminal and run:

    xattr -cr "/Applications/Prompt Library Pro.app"

Then try opening it again.
